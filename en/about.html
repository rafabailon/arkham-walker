The journal

Curiosity, with both feet on the ground.

Arkham Walker is an independent reading journal devoted to uncanny fiction: cosmic horror, gothic mysteries, strange seas, and impossible places. We started it to keep track of the books that made us pause at the window after dark.

Our stories and authors here are fictional examples for this demo. Our reviews aim to be thoughtful, clear about what a book offers, and welcoming to readers arriving from any path.
